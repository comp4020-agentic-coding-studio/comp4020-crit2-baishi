Studio access & membership

Members work independently in Megalo's own facilities year-round, at member rates for classes on top. Membership runs for 12 months. Everyone completes a WHS induction before working alone in either studio.

Two studios

Screen Studio

Vacuum tables, a dedicated fabric printing table, a washout/exposure room for preparing screens, and a heat press.

Press Studio

Relief, etching, photogravure and lithography facilities — the equipment inherited from Studio One that makes Megalo's full range possible.

Membership tiers

  • Membership

    $70 / 12 months

    • Studio access to both Screen and Press studios
    • Member rates on classes and workshops
    • One free access day to learn the space
    • Free entry to the annual Members' Exhibition
    • Can sell through the Print Market and Print Store

  • Concession

    $50 / 12 months

    Same benefits as full membership. For full-time students, Health Care Card, Pensioner Concession Card or Commonwealth Seniors Card holders, and people receiving Centrelink or NDIS support.

  • Friend of Megalo

    $40 / 12 months

    For supporters who don't need studio access: class and partner-business discounts, invitations to the Members' Exhibition and openings, and the newsletter.

All memberships also include a 10% discount at four partner local businesses and Megalo's community newsletter. Renewing an existing membership starts from $50.
