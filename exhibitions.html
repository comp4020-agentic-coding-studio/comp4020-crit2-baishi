Exhibitions & gallery

Megalo's gallery turns over roughly every six weeks, free to visit during office and gallery hours. Its 2026 programme runs from February through November, closing the year with the Wayzgoose print sale.

On now

Rest of the 2026 programme

  • 30 May – 4 July Bootlegs: a fan art exhibition — artists exploring fan art and fan fiction through print.
  • 3 October – 14 November April Phillips, opening Friday 2 October.
  • Friday 20 November Megalo Wayzgoose — the annual print sale and celebration, with artists from around Australia.

Take work home

The Print Store and Megalo Editions sell limited-edition, original prints from local and national artists, alongside printmaking materials, tools and books through the Megalo Emporium.

Megalo also marked its 45th year with a touring survey of its own archive, Canberra vs the Government: Prints from the archive of Megalo Print Studio, curated by Alison Alder — a measure of how long this collection has been building.

This is an independent student redesign made for a university studio unit, not Megalo's official site. For current bookings, opening times and news, go to megalo.org.
