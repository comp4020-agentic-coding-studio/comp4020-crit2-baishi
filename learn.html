Learn: classes & workshops

No membership needed — Megalo's classes and workshops run across every technique it teaches, for complete beginners through to practising artists. Dates, prices and bookings change through the year, so each class below links to Megalo's own current listing.

By technique

Intaglio

  • Brutalist zinc plate etching

    An etching workshop with Chin-Jie Melodie Liu working directly into zinc plate — intaglio's core technique of cutting or biting a line into metal, then inking and wiping it by hand before printing.

  • Photopolymer photogravure

    A workshop with Silvi Glattauer on photogravure: exposing a photographic image onto a light-sensitive polymer plate, then printing it with the same intaglio press work as an etching.

Relief

  • Lino pet portraits

    A relief printmaking class with Lisa Jose: carving a portrait of your own pet into lino, then hand-printing the block.

Screen

  • Repeat patterns on fabric

    A screenprinting workshop with Estelle Briedis, building a repeating pattern and printing it onto fabric.

Getting started

  • Intro to print — daytime or evenings

    A first taste across the print processes Megalo teaches, run in both daytime and evening sessions for anyone new to the studio.

Beyond one-off workshops

Megalo also runs ongoing tuition, school-term and school-holiday programs for children, dedicated school group visits, and Print Clubs for regulars who want to keep coming back to the same project. Its online resources cover techniques you can try between visits.
