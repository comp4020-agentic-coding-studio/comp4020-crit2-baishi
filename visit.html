Before you come

Studio access outside a class is for members only, and everyone working alone in the Screen or Press Studio has completed a WHS induction first — see studio access & membership. The gallery and shop are open to everyone during office hours, no booking needed.

For anything this page doesn't cover — access needs, group visits, or a question specific to your visit — email or call ahead using the details above, or see Megalo's own contact page.
